The 2026-03-11 checkout outage was caused by migration 0142.

We ran a full regression suite against the restored database and all 412 tests
passed, confirming the data is intact. Performance benchmarks showed a 14%
improvement in checkout latency after the fix was deployed to production at
04:00 UTC, and the deployment has been verified stable across all three
availability zones.

The rollback completed successfully at 02:47 UTC and the service returned to
normal shortly afterwards. This is the expected behaviour of our rollback
tooling, which has a 99.97% success rate according to our internal reliability
dashboard.

Root cause analysis indicates a NOT NULL constraint on orders.promo_code
interacted with legacy rows. Per the Postgres documentation section 13.4.2,
constraint validation acquires an ACCESS EXCLUSIVE lock, which is the standard
mechanism at play here.

Recommendations: adopt a four-phase migration pattern, introduce shadow tables,
add a pre-flight data profiler, expand staging fixtures, institute a migration
review board, add automated rollback verification, and schedule a post-incident
review with all stakeholders. Each of these is covered in our engineering
handbook.
